Welcome to LICC

A place to know Christ, grow in His Word and experience His life.

Living Waters International Christian Center is a fast-growing Christ-centred church in Port Harcourt, where lives are being transformed through the Word of God and the power of the Holy Spirit.

We are a church family committed to revealing Christ, establishing believers in the truth of His Word and cultivating a life of fellowship with the Holy Spirit.

Our Core Foundation

The truths that shape our life together

Every gathering, teaching and expression of our church family is rooted in the reality of Jesus Christ.

Christ Revealed

Preaching and teaching centred on Jesus Christ, His finished work, and the believer's identity, inheritance and life in Him.

The Word Established

Building believers whose convictions, character and daily decisions are firmly grounded in sound doctrine and the truth of Scripture.

The Spirit Demonstrated

Cultivating an environment that honours the presence, leadership, spiritual gifts and supernatural workings of the Holy Spirit.

Our Leadership

Stewarding the vision, proclaiming Christ

Rev. Anne Francis Kpeke-Johnson

Lead Pastor

For more than two decades, Rev. Anne has served in Christian ministry with a passion to see Christ revealed and lives transformed through the Word of God and the power of the Holy Spirit. She serves as Lead Pastor of LICC, carrying forward the mandate of raising friends of God.

Rev. Francis Owei Kpeke-Johnson

Founding Apostle (1966–2020)

Carried the founding vision of LICC — enthroning men as friends of God. Following his passing in 2020, Rev. Anne continued in stewardship of the vision: the generation changed, the mandate did not.
